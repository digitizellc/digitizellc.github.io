QM Hosting for your whole company

Y Combinator open-sourced QM - a multiplayer agent harness built for startups, not solo assistants. Every employee gets an isolated workspace. Teams collaborate in Slack and on the web. We deploy and operate it in your Fly.io or AWS account.

  • Live in 48 hours - Slack, web UI, admin panel, and security posture configured
  • Dedicated deployment in your cloud - your data stays in your account
  • Ongoing ops: upgrades, monitoring, rollback, and sandbox management
  • Model-agnostic - Claude, GPT, OpenRouter, and more via your API keys
